Is the universe God?

We believe that the evidence points toward the universe expressing several characteristics of God as understood in common religions. In Christianity God is described as All-Powerfull, All-Knowing, All-Present and Timeless. The universe seems to actually posess several of these characteristics. Firstly the top scientific theorys currently say that first there was nothing and then after a big bang there was everything... if this is true then that likley took infinite or damn near infinite energy to do that, so we will say the universe is All-Powefull or damn near it as the universe did the big bang. Secondly as far as we know the only part of the universe that is aware of itself is humans and some clever self aware animals like elephants which can recognise themselves as existing (tests have been done). So regarding all that is known in the universe (all human + AI + animal conciousnesses put together and even potential aliens conciousnesses out there) the universe is containing all of this conciousness, therefore I could argue that within the universe lies all that is known and therefore the universe is All-Knowing. Another layer to this is whether the universe is aware of every star and planet in existence, to that all I can say is that the universe indiscriminantly applies its laws of physics to all these stars, planets and galaxies and so that could suggest that the universe is aware of all these things but I am not sure. Thirdly regarding the universe being All-Present, it is safe to say that everything that we can scientifically prove exists, exists within the known universe, therefore it is mathematically sound to make the argument that the universe is everywhere (according to our knowledge) and is therefore All-Present Finally, the idea that the universe is Timeless is simple. Either the universe once didn't exist and then one day did exist or it has always been. If the universe has always been that makes it infinitely old which makes its existence unbound by time which makes it timeless. On the otherhand according to the top scientists and evidence currently available, it is believed that if the big bang is true and that first there was nothing then later there was everything, during that time - time itself is said to not apply, in otherwords the universe itself during its beginning is theorised to have been in a "timeless" state, again this argument gives the universe the quality of "timeless". Perhaps it is time that we start worshipping the universe for being provably All-Powerfull, All-Present, All-Knowing and Timeless, personally though, I just need to see a galaxy in a telescope to feel that the universe is worthy of awe and perhaps worship (if one is so inclined to worship something).
