Practice

Hills, fire, star, song

Uniting traditions without a lie underneath them.

The hill

Go high enough to see the city lights. Remember you are one lamp among many, and that looking up is the shared move.

The fire

Sit with people. Heat and faces in the same circle. No performance of “ego death.” Presence. Food if you have it.

The star

Pick a light that does not belong to one tribe. Same sky. Same physics. Same wonder about why anything exists.

The song

Sing something true enough that a Christian, a Muslim, a sceptic and a builder can stand in it without faking. Work the words until they do that.

The stall

Speak on the street the way a man speaks when he is not selling a leash. Invite. Do not trap. Film if you want. Leave when it is done.
