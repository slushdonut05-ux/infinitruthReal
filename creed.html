What we hold

Creed

  1. Our philosophy is grounded in objective reality, we will not believe anything which cannot be proven or at least strongly inferred by the data.
  2. We will not disrespect or put down anyone who believes in a philosophy or religion with little to no evidence, we are not anti falsehood we are just pro- truth, we expect that the truth will prevail when spoken with no need to actively combat falsehood
  3. Every person is born with an empty operating system and the same kind of potential, infinite potential energy. That is the life of life in everyone, that is the source from which we all literally originate. In recognition of this fact it is easy to understand how much we can all be and do when aligned
  4. Religions already point up and suggests the general idea that there is more in life than the materialistic empty persuits. We do not discriminate those who prefer a slightly different translation of the truth of reality, we instead reveal the shared direction uniting all who strive toward God / infinity.
  5. The only way is up: more life, more understanding, more capability, more love, more connection, more cooperation, more freedom and more alignment at the same time.
  6. To be the greatest benefit to the greatest number of people is the most moral way to become rich, poweful and respected. Power is a gun, it is who holds that gun that decides whether humanity ascends to infinity or decends into anarchy.
  7. People who want to leave, leave. No hunting. No White Night. Failure is allowed, it is via this freedom that we shoot our shots at success without fear.
  8. We will always keep humanity in the loop, this philosophy is an ever updating operating system. We will be including any mind smarter than us that can help us build, including General AI when the time arrives..

This is a draft in public. It will get tighter as it gets tested on a stall, not in a comment section.
