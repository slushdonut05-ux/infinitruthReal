What we hold

Creed

  1. Reality is not optional. If it cannot be shown or strongly inferred, do not sell it as fact.
  2. Every person starts with an empty operating system and the same kind of potential. That is the lamp.
  3. Religions already point up. Do not smash the names. Reveal the shared direction.
  4. The only way is up: more life, more understanding, more capability, more love that is real.
  5. A man who gives more may have more. Power is a tool. Who holds it decides if the town lives or dies.
  6. People who want to leave, leave. No hunting. No White Night. Failure is allowed.
  7. Keep humanity in the loop — including any mind smarter than us that we help build.

This is a draft in public. It will get tighter as it gets tested on a stall, not in a comment section.
